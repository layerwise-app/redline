Welcome to Skerry Line

For more than sixty years we have carried islanders, visitors, livestock and the post across the Sound. Whatever brings you aboard, we are glad to have you with us for the crossing.

Our commitment

We reinvest in the routes that keep the outer islands connected, and we are working towards hybrid propulsion on our two newest vessels by the end of the decade.

Travel with confidence

Free changes up to 24 hours before departure, sheltered seating on every deck, and crew who know these waters better than anyone.

Our fleet

MV Kittiwake entered service in 2019 and carries 480 passengers and 62 cars. Her sister ship MV Fulmar joined the route the following spring.

Both ferries have step-free access from the car deck, a galley and a dog-friendly lounge on Deck 4.
